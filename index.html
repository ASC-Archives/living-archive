About this prototype

This is the first working sketch of the ASC Living Archives, built by the ASC Archives Working Group. It brings together two collections recovered in November 2026:

  • PATTERNS, the newsletter edited by Barbara Dawes Vogl: 29 issues, 1998–2009, indexed article by article.
  • The ASC website, 1997–2016: conference sites, papers and abstracts, award citations, news, and the Foundations pages on the history of cybernetics. Four documents survive only as Internet Archive copies and are marked as such.
  • The ASC website, 2016–now, and the conference websites: the 2011 Richmond, 2012 Asilomar, 2013 Bolton and 2014 Washington conference sites with their paper proposals and programs, the smaller 2016, 2017, 2018 and 2019 sites, the Cybernetics of Cybernetics Competition, the 2009 by-laws consultation, and the current site's speaker series and history pages. Participant registration pages are not included.
  • Cybernetics Literacy: the seven essential principles and their concepts from cyberneticsliteracy.org, drawn from the College of Exploration's 2009 survey of ASC members, with each principle's "Try this" exercise. Dorothy searches their full text.

Every item links to its original on asc-cybernetics.org. The people, places and links shown here are laid out so they can load directly into a graph database later, where the archive can grow with every event and conversation.

Ask Dorothy is the archive's guide. For now she searches; later she will answer questions from the archive's own texts and always show her sources.

Corrections and additions are welcome: names, dates, missing issues, things you hold. Please use the feedback page.
